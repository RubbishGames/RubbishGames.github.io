What is ScraplandsSMP?

ScraplandsSMP is a vibrant and friendly Minecraft server focused on a semi-vanilla survival experience. We offer a balanced gameplay environment where players can build, explore, and collaborate without excessive modifications. Our community is welcoming, and we encourage unique builds, exciting adventures, and lasting friendships.

Whether you're a seasoned builder, an intrepid explorer, or just looking for a fun place to hang out, ScraplandsSMP has something for everyone.
